# Tracks

### [1. Uqsa viGrona • A Sting in the Throat](tracks/Uqsa-viGrona.md)
The sting of conscience awakens nightly in the throat; no easy absolution, only relentless good deeds and public confession.

### [2. beAramaya • In Aramaic](tracks/beAramaya.md)
A joyful, swaggering manifesto delivered entirely in Aramaic, daring any imitator to match his sharp, soaring words — with a Zohar‑page‑counting breakdown.

### [3. ⚠️ Batrakh • After You](tracks/Batrakh.md)
A meditation on mortality: the days march to death, the grave is two by three cubits, and only your deeds remain.

### [4. daAna Yachel • For I Can](tracks/daAna-Yachel.md)
The meta‑manifesto: Uqsa can sing this, you can hear it on MP3, but every word stings until your mouth matches your heart.

### [5. vaAttun Amerin • And Ye Say](tracks/vaAttun-Amerin.md)
Hypocrisy exposed: Eden is here on earth, but you wear a white mantle of beautiful lies that covers your eye.

### [6. Zuza • The Coin](tracks/Zuza.md)
A satire of a pious fundraiser whose charity schedule and sob stories are a transparent, mathematical fraud.

### [7. Ruha deShtuta • Spirit of Foolishness](tracks/Ruha-deShtuta.md)
The "as if" philosophy: everything is a hollow performance, and the only product is dried sand poured into empty bottles.

### [8. Masveh deTzidqata • The Mask of Virtue](tracks/Masveh-deTzidqata.md)
Eminem‑style confrontation: "Can I have your attention?" Tear off the mask of virtue, you slaves to your own gold.

### [9. Qisma Rabba • The Great Magician](tracks/Qisma-Rabba.md)
The Great Magician boasts cosmic powers, then trips over a palm leaf and turns gold to sand.

### [10. Sukhleta miFarzela • Iron/Artificial Brain](tracks/Sukhleta-miFarzela.md)
AI panic debunked: the real enslavement is human greed, not bots; Uqsa yells at DeepSeek and GPT, then feels humiliated.

### [11. Ar'a deNura • Land of Fire](tracks/Ara-deNura.md)
A dirge for the "middle east": dancing on the Land of Fire as the world burns, with a George Carlin‑inspired dick‑shaped‑bombs satire.

### [12. ⚠️ Man Hu Da? • Who Is This?](tracks/Man-Hu-Da.md)
A wasp's entrance: who is this? The Sting of Stings, bringing judgment, striking one by one.
